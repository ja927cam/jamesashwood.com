James Ashwood

Software & AI engineer · Cambridge

First-year Computer Science undergraduate at Gonville & Caius, Cambridge. I'm currently on sabbatical from Blueberry Consultants to focus on my degree, and I build tools that make large language models useful — and study the algorithms underneath them.

I like problems where the theory has to survive contact with a real system. Most of my work sits where language models meet actual software: prompt pipelines, validation layers, and tooling that has to be reliable enough for other people to use.

I care about the unglamorous parts of applied AI — evaluation, failure modes, and making the output something a person can trust — and about tooling for structured data.

I studied Maths, Further Maths, Computer Science and Economics at Bishop Vesey's Grammar School. Most of what I actually learned came from building things outside the syllabus: an independent numerical project on ordinary differential equations for Cambridge, a 2D racing simulator with a genetic algorithm written from scratch, and various smaller tools.

I won the Caius Explore essay competition twice, writing about algorithms in data centres and the role of AI in mathematics. I care about formal methods, compilers and systems, and I'm increasingly interested in how verification and machine learning can keep each other honest. Outside of work I race in the Speed Dreams community, write numerical methods for fun, and read too much about motorsport strategy.

This site is a working notebook: ideas I'd like to chase and things I've built. If any of it is interesting, my inbox is open.
